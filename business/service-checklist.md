# Standard clean: service checklist

**Status: current launch version.** This is the single working copy; change it here and let git keep the history. Earlier drafts (14 Oct draft, v2-v5) are archived in `research/our-offering/2026-10-14/`.
- **Prices** are in `price-list.md` and come from `pricing/Pricing_Model.xlsx`. They are launch estimates, adjusted as real job data comes in.
- **Times are estimates.** Every job is timed with start and finish photos (see "Time and photo tracking"). Tasks marked ⏱ are the ones whose time varies most, so watch them in the job data.
- Items we chose a launch default for, and expect to revisit, are listed in **"Launch defaults to revisit"** at the end.

**Built from** the Louisville checklist evidence in `research/checklists/2026-10-14/`. Task sources, URLs, capture dates and quoted text are in `task_matrix_long.csv` and `raw/`.
- "5/5" means all five instant-price competitors include the task in their standard clean: Scrub Lou, Maids in Apron, Super Cleaning, Red Feather and Maid 2 Clean.
- The Evidence column keeps each competitor's own terms (e.g. "deep-only at Scrub Lou"). Our own offering has no deep-clean package.

**How to read each task:**
- **Cleaner does:** the specific action, and what "done" looks like. This column is internal.
- **Customer sees:** the wording for our public checklist.
- **Limit:** what falls outside the task and what happens then. Internal.

## Our offering

One thorough standard clean, priced from the rooms and services the customer selects, plus individually selectable add-ons.
- We don't offer a separate deep-clean package.
- A deep clean is never required, including on the first visit.

**Pricing structure.**
- Customers see room and service prices plus the add-ons they select.
- Cleaner time is used internally to work out cost; it is never shown as an hourly price.
- Partial-home bookings are allowed, including a single room.
- A clearly displayed **minimum booking value** ($150 at launch) applies instead of a minimum room count. It is a price floor only and does not add any cleaning scope.
- **Recurring visits:** the first visit is full price. From the second visit: 20% off weekly, 15% off every 2 weeks, 10% off every 4 weeks. The minimum booking value still applies.
- **Sales tax:** 6% Kentucky sales tax is added at checkout and shown separately.

**Room categories** (prices in `price-list.md`):
- Kitchen
- Full bathroom
- Half bathroom
- Bedroom
- Living / family room
- Dining room
- Office
- Hallway / entry and stairs: selectable areas, cleaned only when the customer selects them
- **Other room**, for any space that doesn't fit the categories above (up to about 250 sq ft; larger spaces count as two). Record its use and approximate size.

There is no separate "Basement" category. Basement spaces are booked as the matching normal room or as Other room, and there is no basement surcharge.

**Pets.**
- At booking, we ask whether pets will be in the home so the cleaner can prepare.
- The customer secures animals where needed and keeps them away from wet floors and cleaning products.
- No pet-care service, no pet surcharge, no allergen-free guarantee.
- Ordinary pet hair on floors and baseboards is part of the standard clean.

## Operating rules for every clean (internal)

1. **Safe working.**
   - Cleaners never stand or climb on furniture, counters, tubs or fixtures.
   - They don't attempt work they can't reach safely with the equipment provided. That work is recorded as *not completed – beyond safe reach*.
   - Reach equipment at launch: extension duster and extension pole only. No step stools or ladders. Nothing about reach or stool height appears in customer-facing wording.
2. **Furniture.** We don't move beds, sofas, dressers, appliances or anything heavy. We clean around them, and under them where our tools reach.
3. **Items on surfaces.**
   - We move a manageable number of ordinary small items (decor, toiletries, remotes, countertop items), clean underneath and put them back where they were.
   - We don't sort, organize or put away belongings, dishes or laundry.
   - A genuinely blocked surface is recorded as *not completed – blocked by items*. It is never skipped silently.
4. **Fixed price and scope.** The price is fixed in advance from the rooms and services selected and the condition the customer disclosed (with photos where we ask for them).
   - If the clean takes longer than we estimated, the price stays the same. That's our estimating error, so it never increases the customer's bill. The time is logged so we can correct future estimates.
   - **Disclosed condition is agreed work.** If the customer disclosed a condition (e.g. heavy stovetop grease or shower buildup) and we included it in the quote, the cleaner does it at the quoted price. Heavy buildup is not a material difference in itself.
   - If the home's condition or the requested scope differs **materially** from what was disclosed, the cleaner pauses before doing any additional work and contacts the office. The customer then chooses either a **revised fixed price** for the full scope or a **reduced scope** at the agreed price. Nothing extra is done or charged without that agreement.
   - "Material difference" means undisclosed condition or scope beyond what was quoted. It is judged against the condition examples (see "Condition examples"), not against how fast the cleaner works. Ordinary dirt is always included.
5. **First visit.** There is no deep-clean requirement. The first visit is priced from the condition and scope we assess from photos or a walkthrough. Time since the last professional clean doesn't change that on its own.
6. **Equipment and tool hygiene.** We bring all supplies and equipment, including the vacuum and mop.
   - **Three separate colour-coded cloth and tool groups:** (a) toilets, (b) other bathroom surfaces, (c) kitchen. They are never used outside their own group.
   - Fresh cloths and fresh mop pads for every home. Used ones are bagged and laundered, not reused that day.
   - The vacuum, mop and caddy are cleaned between visits and recorded on the equipment log.
7. **Product wording.** Customer-facing text says "cleaned". We only say "disinfected" or "sanitized" for a task whose cleaner instructions name the product, the surfaces it's compatible with, and the label's required application and contact time, and only when the cleaner follows those instructions.
8. **Never in scope:**
   - biohazards (pet waste, bodily fluids, mold, pests)
   - hoarding conditions
   - moving heavy furniture
   - hauling trash beyond the household bins
   - exterior windows
   - wet-wiping light bulbs
   - restoring damaged surfaces
   - pet care
9. **Completion record and guarantee.** Before leaving, the cleaner marks every task in every selected room with one of these:
   - **Completed**
   - **Not applicable**: the room doesn't have it (e.g. no ceiling fan).
   - **Not completed**, with a reason: blocked by items, beyond safe reach, fixture unsafe, customer asked to skip, or material condition difference not agreed (rule 4).

   "Out of time" is **not** an acceptable routine reason. If agreed work is left unfinished because time ran out, the visit is recorded as an **incomplete service**. The time overrun is also logged against the estimate.

   The customer receives the record after the visit, listing anything not completed and why.

   **Our remedy for missed or unfinished work (one policy, used everywhere):** the customer chooses either a **free return visit to finish the work** or a **proportionate refund for the work that was missed**. No future booking is required to receive either.
   - **We record it** (incomplete service): the office contacts the customer to offer the remedy. No report from the customer is needed.
   - **The customer finds it:** they report anything missed within 24 hours of the visit, and the same remedy applies. Market norm for a 24-hour re-clean: Scrub Lou, R&R, MaidPro and Maids in Apron.
   - A *not completed* status and its reason record what happened. The label alone doesn't remove a task from the agreed scope, and it doesn't automatically rule out the remedy. The office reviews each not-completed item against what was agreed with the customer (the booking, the quote and any rule 4 agreement) to decide whether the remedy applies.

## Other room (per room)

For any selected space that doesn't fit the categories above (e.g. a basement rec area, sunroom, playroom or gym). It gets the L1–L5 scope plus the "Every selected room" tasks.
- Record the room's **use** and **approximate size** at booking and on the completion record.
- If the room's actual use or condition differs materially from the booking, rule 4 applies.

## Time and photo tracking (every job)

Prices were set from estimated minutes, so every job feeds the real numbers back.

1. **Start photo** of each room before anything is touched, and **finish photo** after the last task. The two timestamps give the room's time, and the photos are the quality record.
2. Label each photo with the room ("Bath 2 - start") and note which cleaners worked in it. Two cleaners in a room for 25 minutes is 50 cleaner-minutes.
3. Also record arrival and departure times, and paid travel time.
4. Add-ons are photographed and timed on their own.
5. Enter the times in the job tracker (`operations/Job_Tracker_TEMPLATE.xlsx`; see `README.md` in this folder). It compares actual minutes with the estimates and suggests updated room prices.
6. Cleaners are paid for the actual time worked. The aim is to meet the quality standard, not to beat the estimate.

**Photo rules.** Customers agree to before-and-after photos at booking. Avoid people, mail, screens and anything identifying. Photos are stored privately, never in this Git repository, and are deleted after 90 days unless a complaint is open.
