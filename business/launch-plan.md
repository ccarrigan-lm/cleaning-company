## 1. Owner decisions and inputs (short)

- [ ] Company name and domain
- [ ] Real cleaner pay rate, and employee vs contractor (put the rate in the pricing model's Inputs sheet and check the margins)
- [ ] Service area (the research priced only ZIP 40217)
- [ ] Working days and hours, and how many jobs a week to start with
- [ ] Read the launch defaults in `README.md` and change any that are wrong

## 2. Business set-up (has lead times: start now)

- [ ] Business entity (e.g. Kentucky LLC), EIN, business bank account
- [ ] Kentucky sales tax registration; accountant confirms the 6% treatment and filing
- [ ] General liability insurance and a bond; workers' comp if cleaners are employees
- [ ] Payroll (employees) or contractor agreements
- [ ] Lawyer review of `policies.md`, plus a privacy notice

## 3. Booking software

Needs: per-room quoting with add-ons and a minimum, online booking and card payment, recurring schedules, cleaner checklists with timestamped photos, customer visit record.

- [ ] Compare 3-4 options against that list and pick one
- [ ] Load the price list, add-ons, discounts and policies
- [ ] Test a booking end to end

## 4. Operations

- [ ] Supplies and equipment kit per cleaner: vacuum, mop and pads, three colour-coded cloth sets (toilet / other bathroom / kitchen), extension duster and pole, caddy, products
- [ ] Laundry routine for cloths and mop pads; equipment cleaning log
- [ ] Walk the cleaners through `service-checklist.md`; fix anything unclear
- [ ] Photo routine: start and finish photo per room, labelled, cleaners noted
- [ ] Copy the job tracker template and start logging from the first job

## 5. Website and listings

- [ ] Website: price list, what's included, policies, booking link. Room-by-room prices and a published checklist are the differentiator: no Louisville competitor prices by room.
- [ ] Google Business Profile
- [ ] Nextdoor and local Facebook groups

## 6. Soft launch

- [ ] 5-10 jobs for friends, family and neighbours at the listed prices
- [ ] Ask each for a review
- [ ] After about 20 jobs: review the tracker Summary, update room minutes and prices, and revisit the launch defaults
