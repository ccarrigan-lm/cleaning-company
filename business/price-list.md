## How pricing works

You choose the rooms you want cleaned and any add-ons. The price is the total of those, fixed before we arrive.

- One thorough standard clean. There is no separate "deep clean" to buy, and we never require one, including on your first visit.
- You can book the whole home, a few rooms, or one room.
- Minimum booking: **$150**.
- If the job takes us longer than we expected, your price stays the same.
- Prices are before 6% Kentucky sales tax, which is shown separately at checkout.

The $150 minimum still applies.

## What's included in every room you select

- Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted, cobwebs removed
- Window sills dusted, blinds lightly dusted
- Accessible baseboards wiped
- Light switches and door handles wiped
- Floors vacuumed and mopped, including ordinary pet hair
- Trash emptied

**Kitchen:** countertops and backsplash, sink and faucet, stovetop including everyday grease and spills, microwave inside and out, appliance exteriors, small appliances wiped, cabinet fronts spot-cleaned, table and chairs, floor.

**Bathrooms:** toilet inside and out, tub and shower including routine soap scum, sink and counter, mirrors, fixtures polished, towels straightened, floor.

**Bedrooms:** furniture, shelves and decor dusted, bed made with the bedding on it, mirrors, room straightened, floor.

**Living, dining, office and other rooms:** surfaces and decor dusted, cushions and throws straightened, glass surfaces spot-cleaned, floor.

The full task-by-task list is in `service-checklist.md` ("Customer sees" column).

## Not included

- Moving heavy furniture or appliances; we clean around and under where our tools reach
- Dishes, laundry, organizing or putting belongings away
- Biohazards (pet waste, bodily fluids, mold, pests) and hoarding conditions
- Exterior windows; hauling trash beyond your household bins
- Upholstery cleaning and furniture pet-hair removal
- Scrubbing grout lines; washing every cabinet front, door and frame; removing stains or scuffs from baseboards. Ask us for a custom quote on these.

## Our promise

- **Fixed price.** If your home's condition is very different from what you told us, we stop and agree a new price or a smaller job with you before doing any extra work.
- **Visit record.** After each clean you get a record of what was done and anything we couldn't do, with the reason.
- **If we miss something,** tell us within 24 hours. You choose a free return visit to finish it, or a refund for the part we missed.
