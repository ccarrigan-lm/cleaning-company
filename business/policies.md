# Customer policies (draft for lawyer review)

**Status: draft.** These are launch defaults written so we can start taking bookings. Have a lawyer review them before they go on the website as terms of service. Items marked *(default)* were chosen to get started and are easy to change. `[Company name]` is a placeholder.

## Booking and price

1. The price is fixed in advance from the rooms and add-ons selected and the condition described at booking. We may ask for photos.
2. Minimum booking value: $150. It is a price floor and adds no cleaning scope.
3. If a clean takes us longer than we estimated, the price doesn't change.
4. If the home's condition or the work requested is materially different from what was described, the cleaner pauses and we contact the customer before doing extra work. The customer chooses a revised fixed price for the full job or a reduced job at the agreed price.
5. Conditions the customer told us about and we quoted for (for example heavy stovetop grease) are part of the agreed job.
6. Recurring service: first visit at full price; 20% off weekly, 15% off every 2 weeks, 10% off every 4 weeks from the second visit. No contract; cancel any time under the cancellation terms below.
7. 6% Kentucky sales tax is added and shown separately.

## Payment *(default)*

1. A card is saved at booking and charged after the clean is completed.
2. Tips are optional and go entirely to the cleaners.
3. Recurring customers are charged after each visit.

## Cancelling and rescheduling *(default)*

1. Free to cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before the appointment.
2. Inside 48 hours: $50 fee.
3. If we can't get in (no one home, no key or code, turned away at the door), we wait 15 minutes and try to reach the customer. After that the visit is charged at 50% of the booked price.
4. If we have to cancel or reschedule, there is never a charge, and we offer the next available time.
5. Arrival is within a 2-hour window; we send a message when the cleaner is on the way.

## Before we arrive

1. Tell us about pets. Secure animals where needed and keep them away from wet floors and cleaning products. We don't provide pet care.
2. Pick up belongings from floors and surfaces you want cleaned. We move a reasonable number of ordinary small items, clean under them and put them back. We don't organize or put things away. A surface that is blocked is recorded on the visit record.
3. For a sheet change, leave clean sheets on the bed. For inside-cabinet cleaning, empty the cabinets first.
4. We bring all supplies and equipment. Tell us at booking about any surface that needs a specific product.
5. Put away valuables, cash and fragile or irreplaceable items.

## What we don't do

Biohazards (pet waste, bodily fluids, mold, pests); hoarding conditions; moving heavy furniture or appliances; climbing on furniture or ladders; exterior windows; hauling trash beyond household bins; restoring damaged surfaces; pet care. Cleaners may decline work that isn't safe.

## Photos

1. Cleaners take before-and-after photos of each room as the quality record. Customers agree to this at booking.
2. Photos avoid people, mail, screens and anything identifying where possible.
3. Photos are stored privately, used only for quality checks, disputes and (with separate written permission) marketing, and deleted after 90 days unless a complaint is open. *(default)*

## Visit record and our guarantee

1. After each visit the customer receives a record of every task: completed, not applicable, or not completed with the reason.
2. If agreed work was missed or left unfinished, the customer chooses a free return visit to finish it or a proportionate refund for the missed work. No future booking is needed to receive either.
3. Where we recorded the problem ourselves, we contact the customer. Where the customer finds it, they tell us within 24 hours of the visit.

## Damage and breakage *(default; confirm against the insurance policy)*

1. Tell us within 48 hours of the visit, with photos.
2. We repair, replace or reimburse items we damaged, up to the limits of our insurance. Items that were already damaged, improperly installed or unstable (for example unsecured shelves or wall hangings) are excluded.
3. We carry general liability insurance and a bond. *(Add the insurer and limits once the policy is in place.)*

## Staff

1. Customers agree not to hire our cleaners directly for 12 months after their last visit. *(default; have the lawyer check enforceability in Kentucky)*
2. We don't tolerate harassment or unsafe conditions; cleaners may leave, and the visit is charged as a late cancellation.
