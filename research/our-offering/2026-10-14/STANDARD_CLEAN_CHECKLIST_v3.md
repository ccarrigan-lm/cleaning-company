# Standard Clean Checklist — v3

**Status:** Approved offering, customer-facing scope defined. Pricing TBD. All items below reflect confirmed decisions as of 2026-10-15.

---

## Core Offering

**One thorough standard clean, priced by selected rooms/services, with individually selectable additions. No separate deep-clean package. No mandatory deep-clean for first visits.**

---

## Confirmed Inclusions (All Selected Rooms)

- **Kitchen:**
  - Microwave interior and exterior
  - Stovetop, including ordinary cooking grease, spills, and appropriate scrubbing
  
- **Bathroom(s):**
  - Shower/tub cleaning, including routine soap scum
  
- **All Rooms:**
  - Wiping accessible baseboards to remove dust, hair, and ordinary surface dirt (not dusting alone)
  - Floor cleaning, including pet hair (no pet surcharge)
  - Making beds with existing bedding

---

## Optional Add-Ons (Individually Selectable)

- Changing sheets (per-bed charge)
- Oven interiors
- Fridge interiors
- Cabinet interiors
- Interior windows/tracks
- Wet-wiping blinds

---

## Excluded from Launch Offering

The following are not available as add-ons in v3:
- Upholstery cleaning
- Couch vacuuming
- Furniture pet-hair removal

---

## Room Categories

Standard room types apply wherever applicable:
- Bedroom
- Bathroom
- Kitchen
- Living room
- Office
- Basement (uses normal categories; no blanket surcharge)
- **Other room** — for spaces not fitting above categories
  - Record: use case and approximate size
  - Treated as standard clean scope unless additional complexity disclosed

---

## Tool Hygiene & Consistency

- **Separate cloths/tools for:** toilet, other bathroom surfaces, kitchen
- **Fresh cloths and mop pads** between every home
- **Equipment cleaned** between visits

---

## Service Boundaries (Internal)

- **Reach/stool policy:** Do not present to customers yet. Retain internal guidance: no climbing furniture; staff against attempting inaccessible work.
- **Time management:** "Out of time" is not a routine justification for incomplete agreed work. Record as incomplete service requiring resolution.
- **Fixed-price protection:** Estimating error does not increase customer bill. Material scope/condition differences require customer agreement before additional work.
- **Floor cleaning:** Applied to selected rooms only. Track once per room without double-counting. Retain room-level timing to support partial-home bookings.
