# Our standard clean – first draft checklist (14 Oct 2026)

**Status: DRAFT for discussion.**
- **No prices are set.** Every price below is provisional until we have cleaner time trials and loaded labour cost.
- **Not tested in a real home.** Tasks marked ⏱ need a time trial before they are priced or promised.

**What it's built from.** The Louisville checklist evidence in `research/checklists/2026-10-14/`. It draws on the 16 Louisville companies; Hello Cleaners (a national platform) is excluded. "5/5" means all five instant-price competitors include the task in their standard clean. Those five are Scrub Lou, Maids in Apron, Super Cleaning, Red Feather and Maid 2 Clean. Where we go beyond the market, the row says so.

**How to read each task.**
- **Cleaner does:** the specific action and the "done" standard.
- **Customer sees:** the wording for our public checklist.
- **Limit:** conditions under which the task is reduced or skipped.

## Ground rules for every standard clean

1. **Reach.** We clean what can be reached standing on the floor or on our own 2-step stool.
   - Higher items get an extension duster only. Nothing is climbed.
   - Market norm: R&R allows a 2-step ladder, Scrub Lou a 3-step ladder. Super Cleaning brings no ladders; Maids in Apron allows no stools at all.
   - *Decision needed:* our stool height.
2. **Furniture.** We don't move beds, sofas, dressers, appliances or anything heavy. We clean around and under them only where reachable (four competitors state this).
3. **Clutter.** We wipe around items left on surfaces and put small items back where they were. We don't pick up, sort or put away belongings, dishes or laundry. Clutter that blocks cleaning is skipped and noted on the visit report.
   - Market: Maids in Apron charges extra time after 2 minutes of de-cluttering per room. Scrub Lou may skip cluttered areas.
4. **Condition assumption.** The standard price assumes a home in normal maintenance condition.
   - If the home needs more time than the estimate, we call before going over. The customer then chooses one of two options: approve extra time at a published rate, or pick priorities.
   - Market: Scrub Lou, Maids in Apron and Super Cleaning each publish a version of this.
   - *Decision needed:* when a first visit must be a deep clean. For comparison, Super Cleaning recommends one if the home hasn't been professionally cleaned in the last 3 months; Maid 2 Clean if it hasn't been cleaned in 30+ days.
5. **Never in scope**, as stated by competitors:
   - biohazards (pet waste, bodily fluids, mold, pests)
   - hoarding conditions
   - moving heavy furniture
   - climbing ladders
   - hauling away trash beyond the household bins
   - exterior windows
   - wet-wiping light bulbs
   - restoring damaged surfaces
6. **Supplies.** We bring all supplies and equipment, including our own vacuum and mop. *Decision needed:* R&R instead uses the client's vacuum and toilet brush to avoid cross-contamination. We need a policy on cleaning or rotating tools between homes.
7. **Walkthrough and guarantee.** Before leaving, the cleaner checks every room against this list. The customer may report anything missed within 24 hours for a free return visit. That is the market norm: Scrub Lou, R&R, MaidPro and Maids in Apron all use 24 hours.

## Decisions to make before we publish

1. **Stool height and reach rule.** This sets W1, W2 and B7.
2. **Inside microwave in standard (K5).** Recommended yes, since 3 of 5 priced competitors include it. Needs a time trial.
3. **Changing linens.** Free when clean sheets are left out, or a paid add-on.
4. **Routine soap-scum removal (B2).** Only Super Cleaning names it explicitly in standard. Decide where "routine" ends.
5. **First-visit rule.** When a deep clean is required, and whether the first visit is priced separately. Maids in Apron and Maid 2 Clean show first-visit and recurring prices separately; The Cleaning Authority makes its first two recurring visits deep cleans.
6. **Tool hygiene between homes** (ground rule 6).

## Time trials needed

Time every ⏱ task, by room type, in at least 3 real homes per size band. Record:
- home condition and floor type
- minutes per bedroom, full bath, half bath and kitchen
- minutes per 500 sq ft of floor (vacuum and mop)
- whether one or two cleaners did the work

Pair the times with the loaded cost per cleaner-hour, supplies per visit and travel. Only then convert this checklist into room and service prices (see `research/analysis/2026-10-14/FINDINGS.md`, "Operational inputs needed").
